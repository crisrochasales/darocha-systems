REGULATORY SCOPE

Safety and authorization come first.

All aircraft maintenance, inspections, alterations and return-to-service approvals must comply with applicable FAA regulations and be performed or approved by appropriately authorized personnel or organizations, including authorized supervision where permitted.

DAROCHA SYSTEMS does not claim FAA repair station certification, mechanic certification, or authority to approve aircraft for return to service on this website. The scope of each aircraft inquiry is assessed before any work is accepted.

For regulatory guidance, consult the Federal Aviation Administration.
